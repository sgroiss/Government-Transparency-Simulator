# Privacy Policy — The Redactor

*Last updated: 2026-07-16*

The Redactor does not collect, transmit, store remotely, or sell any data.
Ironic, given the theme — but true.

- **No analytics, no tracking, no external requests.** The extension makes no
  network connections of any kind.
- **Term list**: stored via your browser's `storage.sync`, which syncs it
  between your own browsers through your own browser account. We never see it.
- **Counters** (per-page and lifetime redaction tallies): stored locally via
  `storage.local` / `storage.session` on your machine only.
- **Page content**: the content script reads page text locally to apply
  redactions. Nothing it reads ever leaves your browser.
